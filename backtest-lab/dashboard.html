The survivorship illusion

same engine, two universes

The first version of this lab used today's Nifty 50 members for all 19 years — the classic backtest sin. Rebuilding the universe from the actual month-by-month index membership (including the stocks that later collapsed out of it) erased about 7 percentage points of phantom CAGR from every strategy, buy & hold included. Hollow bars are the biased numbers; solid bars are the truth. Momentum was hit hardest: in the real universe it rode Suzlon, Unitech and RCom up — and part of the way down.

Robustness — is the edge real?

three independent tests

A genuine edge is a plateau, not a peak: nudge any parameter and the result should barely move. The champion passed all three tests.

2 · Block bootstrap · 2,000 resamples

Daily returns were resampled in 21-day blocks 2,000 times to ask: how much of this result is luck? The answer: the return is uncertain, the profitability is not — every single resample ended positive.

05

Inside the champion

mechanics

All-Weather 40/60 runs two sleeves that win in different weather, rebalanced on every month's last session. The momentum sleeve hunts; the low-volatility sleeve holds the fort.

SLEEVE A / 40% · MOMENTUM

Buy the leaders

Rank every current index member by its 3-, 6- and 12-month return (each skipping the latest month), average the percentile ranks, demand a positive 12-month trend, and hold the top 10 — keeping each until it decays past rank 18 to keep turnover cheap.

SLEEVE A / BRAKE

Breadth scales to cash

When fewer members trade above their own 200-day average, the momentum sleeve scales toward cash (full above 50% breadth, flat below 20%), and idle cash earns the liquid-fund rate. In 2008 this sleeve went to cash while the index halved.

SLEEVE B / 60% · LOW-VOL

Own the calm ones

The 10 least-volatile members of the past year, equal-weighted. Boring by design — this is the betting-against-volatility anomaly, and in India it has been quietly excellent: 16 of 19 years positive on its own.

WHY IT WORKS

Uncorrelated sleeves

Momentum wins trends and crashes in reversals; low-vol lags rallies and cushions crashes. Blended 40/60, the worst drawdown drops to −24.5% against the index's −45.4%, and no 3-year holding period in 19 years lost money (worst: +5%).

Methodology & honesty

read before believing

◆Point-in-time universe

Each month a stock is only selectable if it was actually in the Nifty 50 that month, using official NSE membership history (Bera, figshare 2025). 88 of 100 historical members have full price data.

◆No look-ahead

Signals use only each day's close; execution happens on the next bar. Stocks become eligible only after 252 bars of listed history.

◆Real NSE costs

25 basis points per side — brokerage, STT, stamp duty and impact — charged on all turnover. Idle cash earns 5% p.a. in a liquid fund.

◆IS / OOS discipline

All parameter choices (sleeve mix, band width, horizons) were ranked on 2008–2017 in-sample data only. 2018–2026 was touched once, as validation — and the robustness suite perturbed everything afterwards.

◆Total-return data

Split- and dividend-adjusted daily series for 88 stocks, 2007–2026, from Twelve Data and Yahoo Finance (NSE).

◆Taxes not modelled

Returns are pre-tax. At ~150–300% turnover most gains would be short-term; apply your slab before believing the compounding.

Remaining gaps, declared: 12 delisted ex-members (Satyam, Ranbaxy, HDFC Ltd, JP Associates, Reliance Capital, Cairn and others) have no recoverable price data — their index tenure is excluded, a residual bias that cuts both ways since the list mixes disasters and winners. Membership data ends Aug 2025; later months use the last known membership. Past performance is not a promise; this is research, not investment advice.
